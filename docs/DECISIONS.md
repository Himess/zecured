| D22 | 2026-10-11 | Tier 2 deposit proofs (docs/TIER2.md): a RISC Zero zkVM proof that a v6 Ironwood-only transaction (D5) is in a block committed by a later header (txid path → header `merkleRoot` → ZIP 221 history-tree leaf, path and peaks → that header's `hashBlockCommitments`), creating a note of the vault's amount to the vault's address that the vault can decrypt, in a block at or below the vault's expiry height. | D26 | 2026-10-12 | A deposit proof only reserves the vault, its tag and its account (`ActivationPending`). `finalizeActivation` mints once the relay's best chain has gained `finalizeDepth` (6) headers and `finalizeDelay` (150 s) has passed, with the proof's anchor still on the best chain under `minConfirmationWork` (3.5 M, about ten normal NU7 Testnet blocks); `dropPendingActivation` frees everything once the anchor left the best chain, and the note can be proven again. | D34 | 2026-10-14 | Tier 3 hardening before Deploy 2: each claim of a committed payout names its own output (`SpendCommit.claimOutputs`, distinct indexes, kept in `ClaimPayment.outputIndex`), and F3 counts only what that one action pays the claim's exact receiver, through librustzcash's decryption that checks the note against its `cmx`; nodes never pay one receiver twice in one payout. `finalizeActivation` waits while the slasher has a case open on the vault, and a pending deposit can be neither refunded nor cancelled (regression tests) | Review E1: F3 used to count the largest note a payout paid the claimant's wallet, so two claims to one wallet paid with one note both looked paid.
