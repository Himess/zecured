Built for ZECATHON 2026 on testnets

Your ZEC stays shielded. Only the proof crosses.

Borrow USDC on Base against ZEC that never leaves Zcash’s Ironwood pool, or swap USDC into shielded ZEC. No bridge, no wrapped token, no custodian.

01Deposit

Pay a vault no one holds alone.

Five signers create a fresh 3-of-5 FROST vault for your loan. You pay it from Ironwood like any shielded payment: no amount and no address go public.

  • Zcashan encrypted payment
  • Basenothing at all

02Prove

Only a proof crosses.

A zero-knowledge proof shows your deposit sits in a Zcash block, under a header Base verified itself. zZEC is minted on the proof alone: no signer vouches for it.

  • Zcashnothing new
  • Basea vault id, a standard amount, a keyed tag

03Borrow

Borrow from a brand-new account.

A fresh smart account borrows USDC on Morpho Blue against your zZEC. Its gas is sponsored, so it never held ETH, and nothing on Base points back to your wallet.

04Close

Leave without unshielding.

Repay, or let a market maker close from your collateral. The rest returns to a new Ironwood address, and every payout is committed on Base before it is signed.

05Swap

Buy shielded ZEC with USDC.

Your USDC waits in escrow on Base. It is released only when a zero-knowledge proof shows a market maker paid your shielded address for that very order. No custodian, no signer.

  • Basethe order and a salted commitment, never your address
  • Zcasha shielded payment to you

06Privacy

A bridge leaves four links to you. Zecured leaves none.

Through a typical bridge

  • your transparent address
  • the amount that crossed
  • a mint that matches the deposit
  • the wallet that paid the gas

Through Zecured

  • a proof, a vault id, a standard amount
  • salted commitments, never an address
  • a fresh account that never held ETH
  • nothing that leads back to your wallet

Checked on every deployment: none of 302 identifiers of our own runs appears in 77 events, 55 transactions’ calldata or 177 storage words.

07Trust

We let three signers steal.

On a separate DEMO deployment, three of five signers swept a vault. The borrower’s watchtower caught it two minutes later, a violation proof slashed all five bonds, and the victim was made whole.
