How Zecured works

Every id, height and timing on this page comes from our recorded runs on Zcash testnet and Base Sepolia forks, written down in docs/TESTING.md. Amounts are testnet ZEC (TAZ); 1 ZEC is 100,000,000 zatoshi (zat).

01Overview

Zecured lets you use shielded ZEC on Base without unshielding it. Your ZEC stays in Ironwood, Zcash’s newest shielded pool. Base sees zero-knowledge proofs, salted commitments and a standard-size position, never a Zcash address, transaction or note.

Borrow against shielded ZEC

Pay a fresh 3-of-5 FROST vault from Ironwood. A proof of the deposit mints zZEC, a collateral receipt nobody can transfer, into Morpho Blue, and a fresh, gasless account borrows USDC against it. Close from collateral, or repay and take it all back, to a new shielded address.

Swap USDC into shielded ZEC

Your USDC waits in escrow on Base. A market maker pays your Ironwood address and proves the payment in zero knowledge before the USDC is released. No custodian and no signer; selling ZEC for USDC works the same way, from the command line.

The shared core is a Zcash light client on Base. ZcashHeaderRelay checks Zcash block headers on-chain, proof of work (Equihash) included. ZIP 221 history roots let a proof show that a block is in the chain without saying which one. RISC Zero zkVM programs (“guests”) prove deposits, swap payments, payouts and violations, and ZecuredVersions lists the verifiers each proof is checked with. Any contract can use the same light client to check a shielded Zcash payment, with no bridge.

02Why it matters

Using ZEC in DeFi usually means taking it out of the shielded pool, and three steps then leave four links back to you.

The turnstile

Moving ZEC from the shielded pool to a transparent address publishes that address and the amount that crossed.

What anyone can readt1Q8…Lx9 receives 0.125 ZEC

The wrapper

A lock-and-mint bridge holds your ZEC and mints a token for it elsewhere. You trust it with the coins, and the lock and the mint can be matched by amount and time.

What anyone can readmint 0.125 wZEC to 0x7a3e…41c2

The gas

A new account on another chain needs gas, and the transfer that pays for it ties the account to a wallet you already use.

What anyone can read0x51c0…9e1a sends 0.002 ETH to 0x7a3e…41c2

Zecured has none of the three. The ZEC never leaves Ironwood, nothing is wrapped, and a paymaster pays the account’s gas. Every leak we considered is in the threat model. Shielded ZEC becomes collateral, so holders no longer choose between privacy and liquidity.

03How a loan works

Five steps, seen from both chains at once. The dark panel is the shielded pool, blurred where it is encrypted: switch on the viewing key to read what the borrower can. The light panel is every event Base records, as a block explorer shows it. Gold marks the only things that cross: proofs. The ids, blocks and timings are real, from several recorded runs (TESTING.md), so they do not all belong to one vault.

Step 4 of 5: Borrow

On Zcash

Your ZEC stays put

Nothing moves in the pool. The note sits in the vault; spending it takes three of the five signers, and every spend must be committed on Base first, or it is provable theft.

On Base

Borrow from a fresh account

Your first UserOp deploys a fresh Safe, authorizes Zecured on Morpho Blue and borrows USDC. Our paymaster sponsors the gas, so the account never receives ETH and leaves no funding trail. The USDC comes from lenders on Morpho Blue, who earn its interest: a new market backed by Zcash collateral.

Step 5 of 5: Close, a proof crosses

On Zcash

Paid back in shielded ZEC

Close from collateral: a market maker repays your debt and takes ZEC for it, and the rest goes to a new shielded address of yours, both in one Zcash transaction. Or repay in USDC and take it all back.

  • To the market maker252,500 zatencryptedSent with ovk = None, so not even your viewing key reads it.
  • Transactionsone

πInclusion proof crosses

On Base

Committed before it’s signed

The signers must record every payout on Base before they can sign it (a spend commitment), under a keyed reference and salted hashes of its recipients. Then they confirm it. A payout is final once proven in zero knowledge, or once its dispute window closes undisputed; a proof of its inclusion in a Zcash block settles any dispute.

  1. CloseReleaseyours + the market maker’s = the outstanding
  2. SpendCommittedpayoutRef 0xcdf694fd…inputTag 0xe43d283f…
  3. PayoutInclusionProvenunder a header planned in advance

Measured: the market maker and the borrower were paid in one Zcash transaction, block 4,488,453.

If the price falls

If ZEC falls too far, a liquidator repays part of the debt on Morpho and is paid in shielded ZEC from the vault, with no outgoing viewing key. The vault tracks what is still outstanding, and the rest stays yours, to take back by repaying or closing from collateral. Pre-liquidation is opt-in: your account can set gentler terms of its own, below the market’s liquidation threshold. A liquidation’s payout on Zcash testnet: dc7c669a…, block 4,518,234.

Checked by our leak scanner

The leak scanner gathers the Zcash identifiers of our own runs (txids, note commitments, nullifiers, addresses, keys), then searches every event, every calldata and every storage word of a deployment for them. It prints counts, never values, and fails on any match. The last run looked for 302 identifiers in 77 events, 55 transactions’ calldata and 177 storage words.

05The swap

No custodian, no signer, no wrapped ZEC. Your USDC waits in escrow on Base and moves only on a zero-knowledge proof that a shielded payment for that very order is in the Zcash chain.

Where the liquidity comes from. No liquidity pool and no bridge: a market maker fills each order from its own inventory, ZEC in its shielded wallet and USDC on Base. It quotes the ZEC/USD oracle price plus its spread and signs the quote under EIP-712 for one taker, so nobody else can take it. Anyone with ZEC and USDC can run one (swap/maker); on testnet it is ours, and the price is set by hand (MOCK). On mainnet the price would be Chainlink’s ZEC/USD, checked against API3’s. An order is capped at 200 USDC on testnet and 250 USDC in the mainnet beta.

  1. 1Lock

    BaseYou lock USDC for a market maker’s signed quote. Base stores only keccak256(salt || receiver), never your address.

    ZcashNothing yet.

  2. 2Pay

    BaseNothing. The order waits in escrow.

    ZcashThe maker pays your Ironwood address, with “ZSWP” and the order id at the start of the memo, and no outgoing viewing key.

  3. 3Prove π

    BaseThe maker submits the proof. The contract checks it against a Zcash header the relay verified.

    ZcashThe proof covers the payment, its memo and your salted receiver, under a later header. The payment’s block stays hidden.

  4. 4Release

    BaseA few headers later the USDC goes to the maker. If no proof came in time, it comes back to you.

    ZcashThe ZEC is already in your wallet, shielded.

Measured on a Base Sepolia fork with real Zcash testnet payments: order 0x21660b10, 0.01 ZEC for 11.115555 USDC, paid at 09:52:27, proven at 09:57:39, settled at 10:00:14 UTC. On that fork the payment’s seal came from the MOCK verifier; a real payment seal passes RISC Zero’s router in the fork tests. Any Ironwood address can receive.

06Who you trust

Base never takes the signers’ word. Deposits are proven, payouts are proven, and misbehaviour is proven.

  • One or two signers

    Can’t move anything. In a recorded run two signers, switched to a compromised mode, signed a sweep; the other three refused, and nothing moved.

  • The coordinator

    Holds no key share. It can delay work, but it can’t move ZEC, mint zZEC, or hand you a vault address the signers didn’t sign.

  • Three signers

    Can move a vault’s ZEC, but not quietly. Every payout must be committed on Base before it’s signed, so a theft is provable in zero knowledge, and it slashes the bonds that repay its victims.

  • Signers who stop paying

    Once a signer has acknowledged where a claim goes, a claim left unpaid for 24 hours (72 on mainnet) is paid to its owner from the bonds.

  • All five together

    Could take every vault. That’s the trust that remains, and the reason mainnet needs five independent operators.

The theft demonstration

We let three signers steal, on a separate deployment labelled DEMO, with its own five signers and a 2-minute challenge window instead of 24 hours. Times are UTC, from the run of October 21.

  1. sweep 43ea5e91, block 4,523,730

    Signers 3, 4 and 5 sweep the vault to a wallet of ours. Nothing is committed on Base.

  2. Two minutes later

    The borrower’s watchtower reads the vault with its viewing key and finds its note spent by a transaction no commitment names: violation F1.

  3. 64 s

    The violation’s witness is built from the chain and the viewing key.

  4. 00:04:40

    proveViolation lands. Its journal holds no Zcash transaction, note or address, and the vault freezes at once.

  5. 00:06:47

    executeSlash, once the 2-minute window is over: all five bonds are slashed.

  6. 00:16:53

    The incident closes at $1,265 per ZEC. The slashed bonds repay the victim’s debt and retire its zZEC.

  7. 3 → 12.65 USDC

    The victim’s account after it withdrew its equity: the full value of its 0.01 ZEC.

On this fork the violation’s seal came from the MOCK verifier; a real F1 seal passes RISC Zero’s router in the fork tests. On Zcash testnet one Equihash ASIC out-mines the whole network about 40 times over, so every guarantee here that rests on proof of work is demonstrative. The five demo signers are run by us: the bonds show the mechanism, not independent economic security.

07A judge’s questions

Isn’t this just a multisig?
It’s a FROST threshold vault: one shielded address whose signatures look like a single signer’s, and every signer enforces a written policy on every payout. Two compromised signers can’t move funds (see Who you trust).
Is zZEC a wrapped ZEC? Is this a bridge?
No. zZEC is a non-transferable collateral receipt, minted only into Morpho, that no account ever holds; each position’s zZEC equals what its own vault holds. Neither product moves ZEC off Zcash: the swap pays real shielded ZEC and releases escrowed USDC on a proof of that payment.
How do I pay interest without linking my identity?
Close from collateral: a market maker repays the debt on Morpho and takes ZEC from the vault, and the rest returns to a new shielded address in the same transaction. No outside funds ever touch your account.
How long does a loan take, and what does it cost?
About eight minutes in our browser run: the signers saw the deposit after 2 min 50 s, its proof was on Base after 4 min 25 s (MOCK verifier on that fork; a real proof adds its GPU and Groth16 time), and the loan was live after 7 min 56 s. The account’s gas is sponsored; the vault keeps a 0.5 % opening fee in ZEC (waived below 20,000 zatoshi), and the Morpho market sets the interest.
Isn’t a Zcash light client on Base a bridge?
It is the opposite of a lock-and-mint bridge: no asset moves through it and nobody holds coins for it. It lets Base read Zcash headers, their proof of work checked on-chain, and verify zero-knowledge proofs about shielded transactions under them. Zecured uses it to mint a non-transferable receipt and to release escrowed USDC; any contract can use it to check a shielded payment.

09Toward mainnet

The mainnet beta changes configuration, not code. Full mainnet also needs the designs not yet built (a dispute bond, per-signer reimbursement, the fixes in Known limitations), and what code can’t bring: independent operators, real bonds and liquidity, and an audit.

  1. Done

    Checked against mainnet

    The v3 suites’ 533 contract tests on mainnet’s Morpho Blue and RISC Zero router, the real seals verified there, the relay run on 1,600 real Mainnet headers, the price feed read from live Chainlink and API3.

  2. About November 5, 2026

    NU7 on Zcash Mainnet

    The relay’s activation height, the guests’ branch ids and the off-chain stack follow the upgrade.

  3. Then

    An invite-only beta

    Unaudited and labelled so: a 1 ZEC cap, vaults of 0.05 and 0.1 ZEC, five bonds of 300 USDC, and exits that never stop.

  4. After an audit

    Mainnet

    Five independent operators with bonds of 50,000 USDC each, a 100 ZEC cap and 72-hour windows, after an audit of the contracts, the guests and the signer policy.

The vault design is written up as a draft ZIP, Shielded Collateral Vaults, for any wallet or chain to build on. What is still open, and how each gap would close, is in Known limitations and their designs.

10Run it yourself

No deployment is kept running through the judging period. Two ways to see it: the app’s replay plays a whole loan and a swap in your browser, with sample data and nothing to install; or run everything on your machine (five signer processes, the coordinator, the contracts on a local fork of Base Sepolia and the app, paying real Zcash testnet notes from the faucet). Plan an afternoon for the local run; the README walks through it.
